# Observability

Observability helps platform teams understand AI traffic behavior, reliability, cost, and governance outcomes.

## Target Signals

- Request count, latency, and error rate.
- Policy allow, deny, and review decisions.
- Provider and model routing decisions.
- Token estimates and cost allocation metadata.
- Safety and responsible AI review markers.

## Early Scope

The first iteration documents observability intent only. Provider-specific dashboards and alarms are future work.
